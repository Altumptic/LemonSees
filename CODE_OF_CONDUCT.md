# Code of Conduct

LemonSees is a security tool built by people who care about getting things right. We want the community around it to be
respectful, constructive and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background, experience or role.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps: assuming good intent, giving and accepting specific feedback, crediting others, admitting
mistakes, and helping newcomers.

Behaviour that does not belong: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information (including IP addresses, logs or credentials from their systems), and sustained disruption.

Security research is welcome when it is responsible. Do not test systems you do not own or have permission to test.

## Scope

These standards apply in the repository, issues, discussions, pull requests and any official LemonSees channel.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Altumptic/LemonSees/security/advisories/new) form, selecting
"Other" and writing "Code of Conduct" in the title. Maintainers review every report and will respond as soon as possible.
Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take action proportionate to the issue, from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from
project spaces. Decisions are made by maintainers and are final for the project space.
