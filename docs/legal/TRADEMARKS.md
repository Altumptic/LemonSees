# Names and logos

The source code of LemonSees is free software under the [MIT License](../../LICENSE): you may use, copy, modify, fork and
redistribute it. The licence covers the **code**. It does not give you the right to use the **names and logos** of the
project as if they were yours.

This page is the maintainers' policy, written in plain language. It is not legal advice, and it does not claim any
registered trademark: at the time of writing, no registration is claimed. The maintainers update this page if that changes.

## What this covers

- the names **LemonSees** and **Altumptic**;
- the logos and brand images in this repository (for example under `.github/assets/`, `buildResources/` and `public/`).

## What you may do without asking

- Say that you use LemonSees, that your tool works with it, or that you wrote about it, with the name spelled correctly
  and, where reasonable, a link to this repository.
- Show the logo to refer to the project in an article, talk, review, or list of tools, without suggesting that the
  project endorses you.
- Redistribute **unmodified** official releases under their name.
- Fork the code and publish your fork on GitHub under the fork's own name.

## What you may not do

- Present a **modified** build as "LemonSees" or as an official release, or distribute it under the same name and logo in
  a way that could confuse users about who made it.
- Imply that Altumptic or the LemonSees maintainers sponsor, endorse or support your product, service or company.
- Use the names or logos in your own product name, domain name, app-store listing or company name.
- Use the logo in a way that makes your own product look like the official one.

If you distribute a modified version, give it a different name and logo, and say clearly that it is based on LemonSees.
Keeping the copyright and licence notices (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`) is required by the MIT License in any case.

## How to tell an official release

Official releases are published on [github.com/Altumptic/LemonSees](https://github.com/Altumptic/LemonSees/releases) and
carry a Sigstore signature and a build-provenance attestation. [security/VERIFY.md](../security/VERIFY.md) shows how to check
them. Builds from any other source are not official, even if they use the name.

## Permissions and questions

If you want to use the names or logos in a way not covered above, ask first in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Altumptic/LemonSees/discussions).
